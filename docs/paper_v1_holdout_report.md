# paper-v1 holdout report — 2026-09-27

## Execution status

The frozen `paper-experiment-v1` protocol was executed once on all 30 reserved scenarios H01–H30. No algorithm, scenario, endpoint or seed-manifest changes were made after the `paper-experiment-v1` tag. The run produced 1,080 event rows: 30 scenarios × 4 algorithms × 9 events. All 30 scenarios completed.

Primary add-event endpoints were generated by `SummarizeAddRecovery`; paired run-level tests were generated by `RunPaperStatistics`. Event rows inside a scenario are not treated as independent replicates.

## Main results

| Method | Add feasible recovery | Penalized FE-to-feasibility | Mean add lateness | Mean response time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Restart PSO | 0.094 | 18.322 | 116.684 | 1.475 |
| Warm-PSO | 0.472 | 11.117 | 23.318 | 1.405 |
| Warm-ALNS | 0.589 | 8.472 | 8.623 | 1.409 |
| EAT-PSO | 0.539 | 9.722 | 13.163 | 1.421 |

Lower is better for penalized FE, lateness and response time; the censor value is `maxFE+1 = 19`.

## Paired tests

The predeclared Holm-corrected paired Wilcoxon comparisons are in `results/paper_v1_holdout_stats.csv`:

- EAT-PSO vs Restart PSO: significant improvement on add recovery, penalized FE and lateness after Holm correction.
- EAT-PSO vs Warm-PSO: recovery and lateness differences are not Holm-significant at 0.05; penalized FE is significant at the corrected threshold in the current output.
- EAT-PSO vs Warm-ALNS: EAT-PSO is not superior. Warm-ALNS has higher add recovery, lower penalized FE, and lower lateness; the corresponding paired differences are unfavorable to EAT-PSO.
- Response time differences are not Holm-significant against the two warm baselines in the current output.

The correct claim is therefore **not** “EAT-PSO is the best dynamic routing method.” The defensible claim is narrower: EAT-PSO substantially improves over restart PSO, is competitive with Warm-PSO, but does not outperform the independent Warm-ALNS-inspired baseline on the primary holdout endpoints.

## Research decision

Do not add local search, tune EAT parameters, or reopen the holdout. If this work is written as a paper, present Warm-ALNS as a strong comparator and state the trade-off honestly: the proposed reconstruction PSO is a clear improvement over restart and offers rapid recovery, but it is not universally dominant over a routing-specific adaptive-neighborhood baseline.
